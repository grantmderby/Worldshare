WorldShare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

WorldShare is a Minecraft mod. This page explains, in plain language, what it accesses, where that data goes, and what the developer can and can't see.

What WorldShare accesses

When you connect your Google account, WorldShare requests permission to read and write files inside a Google Drive folder you explicitly choose, using Google's Drive API with the narrowest scope Google offers for this purpose (drive.file). It does not request access to your entire Drive, your Gmail, or any other Google data.

The only data WorldShare uploads is your Minecraft world's save files — region files, player data, and related world state — so that another player can download and continue the same world.

Where your data goes

Directly from your Minecraft client to Google Drive, using your own Google account. WorldShare does not operate a server of its own and does not route your world data through any server the developer controls. The developer of WorldShare has no access to your Drive files, your world data, or your Google account.

What's stored locally

Your OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on your own computer, inside your Minecraft installation's config folder. They are never transmitted anywhere except directly to Google, and never bundled into any file you share with anyone else.

Revoking access

You can revoke WorldShare's access to your Google account at any time from Google's own account settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Doing so does not delete anything already in your Drive folder — it only stops WorldShare from being able to read or write to it until you sign in again.

Questions

WorldShare's source code is public — see the GitHub repository for exactly how it uses the Drive API.
