WorldShare

Share one Minecraft singleplayer world with someone far away, without running a dedicated server.

WorldShare is a Minecraft mod that lets two players keep a single singleplayer world in sync over Google Drive, with session locking so you never overwrite each other's progress. Either of you can open the world solo, and when you're both online at the same time, you play together live over a peer-to-peer relay.

What it needs from you: a Google account, and permission to read and write files in a Google Drive folder you choose. See the privacy policy for exactly what that means.

No dedicated server, no third-party hosting — your world lives in your own Google Drive, in a folder you pick, and only the people you've shared that folder with can reach it.
